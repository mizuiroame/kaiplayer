# Controls

[Back to the README](../README.md)

KaiPlayer has five tabs: **Home**, **Library**, **Playlists**, **Queue** and
**Now Playing**. Press **ZL** / **ZR** to move between them. Starting a song
keeps you where you are; open Now Playing with **ZR** or by tapping the
player bar.

## Buttons

| Button    | Library                      | Playlists                    | Queue                      | Now Playing                |
|-----------|------------------------------|------------------------------|----------------------------|----------------------------|
| ↑ ↓ / LS  | Move (↑ from the first row selects **‹ Back**) | Move (same)     | Move                       | ↑ shows or hides the lyrics (when the song has them) |
| →  / ←    | → adds the row to a playlist | → removes the song (inside a playlist, after you confirm) | → adds the song to a playlist | Rewind / fast-forward 10 s |
| A         | Open an artist, album or folder / play from song (or press the selected **‹ Back**) | Open playlist / play from song | Play this track | Pause / resume |
| B         | Back (up a page or a folder) | Back to the playlist list    | Back to Library            | Add the song to a playlist |
| X         | Play the artist, album, list or folder | Play the playlist            | Shuffle on / off           | Shuffle on / off           |
| Y         | Shuffle it                   | Shuffle the playlist         | Repeat: off / queue / song | Repeat: off / queue / song |

In every tab:

| Button  | Action |
|---------|--------|
| ZL / ZR | Previous / next tab |
| L / R   | Previous / next track |
| −       | Pause / resume |
| RS ← →  | Rewind / fast-forward 10 s |
| RS ↑ ↓  | Previous / next page of a list |
| RS      | In the menu, Settings and the keyboard, moves like the D-pad |
| +       | Open the menu. Press **+** again to exit KaiPlayer: it asks you to confirm. |

If a song has played for more than 3 seconds, **L** restarts it. Otherwise
**L** goes to the previous song.

On Home, use the D-pad or the left stick to move between cards and sections,
**A** to open one, **X** to play it and **Y** to shuffle it. See
[Home](library.md#home). With a [network folder](network-folder.md) or a
[Navidrome server](navidrome.md) set up, **X** on Home and on Library's first
page switches the library to the next one instead: SD card, network folder,
Navidrome (the pill at the top shows the **X**).

## The player bar

The player bar under Home, Library, Playlists and Queue has ⏮ ⏯ ⏭, Shuffle
and Repeat. To use them with the buttons, press ↓ on the last row of the
list: ← → choose a control, **A** presses it, and ↑ or **B** go back to the
list. **−**, **L** and **R** also pause and change the song from anywhere.

## The + menu

**+** opens a menu with actions for the current tab:

| Tab         | Menu items |
|-------------|------------|
| Playlists   | New playlist, rename or delete a playlist; inside a playlist, move or remove a song |
| Queue       | Save the whole queue as a playlist |
| Now Playing, an Internet Archive page | About this song: its artist, license and page on archive.org (for the Archive's songs) |
| All         | Update the library (not for the Internet Archive), Go to the … (the next source), Turn screen off (while music plays), Settings, and Exit KaiPlayer (asks you to confirm) |

**+ → Update the library** checks the music folder again after you add
music (see [Library](library.md#library)). **+ → Go to the … library**
switches to the next source, as **X** does on Home, and opens Library on it;
the item says which one (**SD card**, **network folder**, **Navidrome** or
**Internet Archive**).

Adding songs to a playlist isn't in the menu: use the add-to-playlist
buttons, a list with a small plus (see [Playlists](playlists.md)).

Use ↑ ↓ and **A** to choose, and **B** to close the menu. To exit, press
**+** twice and confirm with **A** (or press **+** a third time).

## Touch screen

Everything can also be done by touch:

- **Tabs:** tap a tab name to open it.
- **Lists:** tap a song to play it, or a folder or playlist to open it. Drag
  up or down to scroll: the list follows your finger and glides on when you
  let go, as on a phone, and a touch stops it. **‹ Back** (next to the folder
  path, or the playlist name) goes up a folder or back to the playlist list.
- **Home:** tap a card.
- **Add-to-playlist buttons:** every song and folder row ends with a button
  (a list with a small plus) that adds it to a playlist, and so does **Add
  to playlist** in Now Playing.
- **Player bar** (below Home, Library, Playlists and Queue): tap ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ to go
  back, play or pause, and skip. Tap **Shuffle** or **Repeat** to change
  them, or anywhere else on the bar to open Now Playing.
- **Now Playing:** tap ⏮ ⏯ ⏭, tap the progress bar to jump to that point
  (or drag along it and let go where you want), and tap the Shuffle and
  Repeat buttons.
- **Menu:** tap the ☰ button or the KaiPlayer title in the top left to open
  the + menu. Tap an item to choose it, or outside the menu to close it.
- **Library source:** tap the pill next to the KaiPlayer title to switch to
  the next source (the Internet Archive is always one), and
  the pill at the top right (while a library on the network is read) to see
  that one.
